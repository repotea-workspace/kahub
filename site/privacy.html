Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 · https://kahub.in

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information necessary to provide and improve our products, including account contact details (such as your email address), workspace content you choose to save, and basic usage diagnostics.

2. How We Use & Protect Your Data

  • Service Delivery: To operate, maintain, and support your Kahub workspace and features.
  • Privacy by Default: Your private workspace documents and notes are never sold to third parties or used to train public AI models.
  • Security: All data is encrypted in transit and at rest using industry-standard security practices.

3. Data Retention & Deletion

You may export or delete your workspace content at any time, or request complete account deletion by contacting us at hello@kahub.in.

4. Contact

For any privacy-related questions or requests, please reach out to hello@kahub.in.
