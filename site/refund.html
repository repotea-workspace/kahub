Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: November 1, 2026 · https://kahub.in

1. Digital Service Access

Kahub provides online software products and digital workspace services. Account features are activated digitally upon registration or plan selection.

2. Cancellations

You may cancel a paid workspace plan at any time through your account settings or by contacting us by email. There are no cancellation fees, and your access continues until the end of the current billing period.

3. Refunds

If you are not satisfied with a paid Kahub product, you may request a full refund within 14 calendar days of purchase. Approved refunds are returned to the original payment method within 5–10 business days.

4. Contact

To request a refund or assistance with your account, please email hello@kahub.in.
